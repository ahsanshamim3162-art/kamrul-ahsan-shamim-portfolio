The Credibility of Influencer Marketing and Its Impact on Consumer Purchase Decisions

Exploratory quantitative research examining how influencer credibility factors can affect consumer purchase behavior.

n = 103 · Cronbach’s Alpha = 0.676 · R² = 0.432

Experience and influencer trustworthiness showed significant positive effects on purchase behavior, while perceived credibility and attractiveness were not statistically significant in the regression model.
