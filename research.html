THE CREDIBILITY OF
INFLUENCER MARKETING.

The credibility of influencer marketing and its impact on consumer purchase decisions. An exploratory quantitative research project examining how influencer-related factors can influence consumer purchase behaviour.

103 RESPONDENTS
0.676 CRONBACH'S ALPHA
0.432 R² MODEL
2025 NORTH SOUTH UNIVERSITY
01 / RESEARCH QUESTION

What are the factors of influencer marketing credibility that impact consumers' purchase decisions?

The study investigates how consumers evaluate influencers and whether factors associated with influencer credibility are related to purchase behaviour.

02 / RESEARCH DESIGN

Methodology

Nature: Exploratory quantitative research

Sample: 103 respondents

Sampling: Non-probability sampling

Time horizon: Cross-sectional

Target context: Consumers, with focus on Gen Z

03 / ANALYTICAL PROCESS

From questionnaire to evidence

1. Questionnaire development and refinement

2. Data collection from 103 respondents

3. Reliability testing

4. Pearson correlation analysis

5. Multiple regression analysis

6. Hypothesis testing and interpretation

04 / CONCEPTUAL MODEL

Hypotheses

The research examined four influencer-related factors in relation to consumer purchase behaviour.

H1

Influencer experience has a positive effect on consumer purchase behaviour.

H2

Perceived influencer credibility has a positive effect on consumer purchase behaviour.

H3

Influencer attractiveness has a positive effect on consumer purchase behaviour.

H4

Influencer trustworthiness has a positive effect on consumer purchase behaviour.

07 / MODEL PERFORMANCE

43.2% of the variance explained

The regression model produced an R² value of 0.432, meaning the variables included in the model explained 43.2% of the variance in purchase behaviour.

The result suggests that influencer-related characteristics can provide meaningful explanatory power when studying consumer purchase behaviour, while also indicating that other factors outside the model contribute to the decision.

08 / BUSINESS TAKEAWAY

Reach is not the whole story.

The findings suggest that influencer selection should consider relevant experience and trustworthiness rather than relying only on audience size, appearance or general popularity.

For marketers, the practical implication is to evaluate whether an influencer is perceived as knowledgeable, experienced and trustworthy within the relevant product or category context.
